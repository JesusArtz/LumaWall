# Community code of conduct

LumaWall is built in the open. Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal attributes.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish private information.
- Respect creators' licenses and do not share copyrighted wallpaper assets.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report
conduct concerns privately through the repository's security contact channel.
